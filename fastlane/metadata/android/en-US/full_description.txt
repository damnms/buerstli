<p>The official app of your toothbrush wants an account, a phone number and a verification code. To adjust a toothbrush. Bürstli doesn't. It talks to your brush over Bluetooth, and that's it – the app doesn't even have permission to access the internet.</p>

<p><b>Supported brushes</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Laifen Wave (LFTB01)</b> – tested on a real brush: battery, all four modes with strength, range and speed, switching modes, brushing time, 30-second nudge, high frequency (the hidden fourth mode), start and stop from the phone, flight mode, firmware version.</li>
<li><b>Laifen Wave Pro (LFTB02)</b> – experimental, listens only: battery, brushing time, brushing or idle, pressure warning, 30-second nudge, high frequency, flight mode and its Pro features.</li>
<li><b>Laifen Wave SE (LFTB01-SE)</b> – experimental, listens only: battery, modes, brushing time, brushing or idle, 30-second nudge, high frequency, flight mode.</li>
<li><b>Laifen Kids TC51 (LFTB01-C)</b> – experimental, listens only: battery, brushing time, brushing or idle, child lock, 30-second nudge, high frequency, flight mode and more.</li>
<li><b>Philips Sonicare</b> (classic models such as DiamondClean, Prestige, ExpertClean, Kids) – experimental. Confirmed on a real DiamondClean 9000: battery, status, mode, intensity, brushing time and brush head wear. New and untested: model number, firmware, brushing history, pressure warning, setting the brush's clock, switching mode and intensity.</li>
<li><b>Oral-B</b> (iO, Genius, Smart, Pro, Triumph) – experimental, listens and connects only briefly for what it doesn't broadcast: status, mode, brushing time, pressure, mouth area, battery, brushing history and when the brush head is due.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your brush isn't supported yet?</b> Bürstli recognizes the Philips Sonicare 7100, Oclean and Xiaomi brushes – and any other brush you simply pick from a list of nearby devices. Bürstli then records a report in about a minute – it only listens, nothing on the brush is changed. Send it to the developers by messenger, e-mail or on GitHub, no technical knowledge needed.</p>
