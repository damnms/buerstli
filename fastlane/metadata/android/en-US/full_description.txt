<p>The official app of your toothbrush wants an account, a phone number and a verification code. To adjust a toothbrush. Bürstli doesn't. It talks to your brush over Bluetooth, and that's it – the app doesn't even have permission to access the internet.</p>

<p><b>Supported brushes</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Laifen Wave (LFTB01)</b> – tested on a real brush: battery, all four modes with strength, range and speed, switching modes, brushing time, 30-second nudge, high frequency (the hidden fourth mode), start and stop from the phone.</li>
<li><b>Philips Sonicare</b> (classic models such as DiamondClean, Prestige, ExpertClean, Kids) – experimental and read-only: battery, status, mode, intensity, brushing time and brush head wear.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Your brush isn't supported yet?</b> Bürstli recognizes other Laifen models, Philips Sonicare 7100 and Oral-B, and can record a report in about a minute – it only listens, nothing on the brush is changed. Send it to the developers by messenger, e-mail or on GitHub, no technical knowledge needed.</p>

<p><b>Honest about everything</b></p>
<ul>
<li>A setting only counts as saved once the brush itself reports the new value.</li>
<li>Experimental drivers only read, they never change anything on your brush.</li>
<li>"For nerds: log" shows every Bluetooth step. It stays on your phone.</li>
<li>Written with the help of an AI assistant and tested step by step on a real brush.</li>
</ul>
